🎲 Classic Parcheesi Game

Click the dice in your base, or use Roll dice, on your turn. The dice jump to the center to roll. Then choose a legal move from the right panel. Play each die in sequence; both can move the same piece when each landing is legal. Pieces travel counterclockwise and turn into their own HOME lanes. Two pieces of one color form a blockade.
