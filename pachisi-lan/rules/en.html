Parcheesi · Game rules

Goal and setup

For 2–4 players. Each player has four numbered pawns in their coloured starting area. Move all four HOME first to win. Each player rolls two dice; the highest total starts (tied leaders roll again). Play continues to the left.

Entering

Enter a pawn on your coloured entry space using a five: one die showing 5, or both dice totalling 5 (4+1 or 3+2). You must enter whenever possible. If entry is blocked, use the five for another legal move. Entry consumes the selected die or dice.

Moving

Move counterclockwise. Use each die as a separate move; the same pawn may use both dice, but each intermediate landing must be legal. You must move whenever possible and use both dice if possible. Otherwise use either playable die. No more than two pawns of one colour may occupy a space.

Doubles

Matching dice grant another roll after movement and bonuses. If all four pawns are already entered, use the two top and two bottom faces, totalling 14. Allocate all four numbers to one or more pawns; if all four cannot be used, move none and roll again. On your third consecutive doubles, do not move: return your pawn closest to HOME (including the home lane, excluding finished pawns) to its starting area and end your turn.

Captures and safety

Land on an opponent on an ordinary space to send it back to its starting area. Marked safety spaces cannot be shared by different colours and protect pawns from capture. Exception: entering onto your own entry space captures a single opponent there. Home lanes are private.

Blockades

Two pawns of the same colour on any path form a blockade. No pawn may land on, pass or capture a blockade, including its own. A blocked entry prevents entering. You may have to break your own blockade when no other move is possible. The two members cannot move together to form another blockade on a new space during the same turn.

HOME and bonuses

After going around the board, take your own seven-space home lane. HOME counts as a space and requires an exact count. Each capture earns a separate 20-space bonus; each pawn reaching HOME earns a separate 10-space bonus. After using the dice, move any single pawn the full bonus count, or forfeit that bonus if impossible. Bonuses cannot be split, combined or used to enter. Complete bonuses before the next roll, including doubles. The first player with all four pawns HOME wins.
