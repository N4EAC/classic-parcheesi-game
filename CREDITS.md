The project-supplied victory recording is included as `pachisi-lan/winner.wav`.

## Board and rules

The board, wooden appearance, dice, pieces, and animations are drawn programmatically by the game. The rules pages are independently written summaries based on the supplied 2007 Winning Moves Parcheesi rule sheet, translated into nine languages. The original PDF is not included in this repository.
